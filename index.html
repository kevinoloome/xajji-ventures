Our Story

Built on Trust & Reliability

Xajji Ventures is a leading transport, delivery, and contractual services provider based in Nairobi, Kenya. Located along Lunga Lunga Road at the National Cereal Produce Board Building, we specialize in efficient, secure, and timely logistics.

With a diverse fleet of trucks, vans, and motorcycles, we ensure seamless transportation, last-mile delivery, and cargo handling using cutting-edge tracking technology for real-time updates.

Our commitment to reliability, innovation, and customer satisfaction enables us to offer cost-effective, flexible logistics solutions that optimize supply chains across Kenya.
